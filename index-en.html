Ref. 2026-001 · Price on request

At via Machiavelli 18-22, in one of the most established areas of Lesmo, we are offering a residential building renovated in 2026, set over the ground and first floors with a total floor area of 480 m². The property comprises four residential units — three four-room apartments and one two-room apartment — ready to move in, designed for contemporary, efficient and practical living, with well-balanced spaces.

The interiors are laid out to make the most of the natural light that floods every room, and to provide an easy, practical layout. The eat-in kitchen is designed as a place to gather, while terraces, balconies and the large shared garden take home life outdoors. A double garage and six outdoor parking spaces complete the package, features of real everyday value.

The apartments have independent heating and are in energy class A4, with underfloor heating and pre-installation for air conditioning. Heating and cooling are provided by a heat pump; the building also features photovoltaic and solar thermal panels. Outside, two charging points allow electric cars to be charged.

From here you can walk in a few minutes to the Town Hall and municipal offices, the post office, Farmacia Ambrosiana in via Marconi 29 and shops for everyday needs. The state comprehensive school in via Donna Rosa 13, with primary and lower secondary classes, is within walking distance; the town also has the Casati nursery school and the Fate e Folletti day nursery.

For getting around, Lesmo station on the Seregno–Bergamo line and the provincial bus network provide connections to nearby towns, while the SP7 provincial road gives quick access to Monza and Vimercate. The area keeps a human scale, with essential services, green spaces and sports facilities close by.

Thanks to its four independent units and outdoor spaces, the building is also a solid basis for a cohousing project or for an extended family wishing to live close together: parents, children and grandparents in the same building, each in their own home.

A building designed for families looking for generous, well-organised spaces, for professionals who value energy efficiency and good connections to Milan and Monza, and for investors interested in a new product that is ready to market.

Shared living

Four independent homes,
a possible community

Among friends, among families or within the same family

The building at via Machiavelli 18-22 lends itself to a small-scale cohousing project. Four independent homes, a small community and generous outdoor spaces are a concrete basis for anyone who imagines living close to other families without giving up their privacy.

Cohousing combines independent homes with shared spaces and services, organised according to rules agreed together. With four units the community is small enough to encourage direct relationships and simple decisions, while each household keeps full independence in its own home.

It is also the ideal home for an extended family: parents, grown-up children, grandparents, brothers and sisters with their own families can live side by side, each in their own home. Everyone helps out in everyday life (the children with their grandparents, a hand for older relatives, Sunday lunch in the garden) without giving up their own front door.

A building of four independent units, designed for those who imagine shared living, with other families or with their own, without giving up their independence: a garden to enjoy together, outdoor spaces for spending time in company and the freedom to organise life together according to shared rules.

How we support you

  1. Project presentation

    We show you the building, which spaces can be shared and which remain private.

  2. Meeting the future residents

    Whether you are a group of friends, several families or an extended family, we organise meetings to decide together how to live in the building.

  3. Rules and shares

    We help you agree on the use of the garden and outdoor spaces, parking, the division of costs, guests, pets and how decisions are made.

  4. Written agreements

    We support you right through to drafting the agreements and signing the purchase deed for each unit.

The building consists of independent residential units with shared parts. The cohousing project is not yet in place: it is an opportunity that future owners can build together. In Italy, shared parts are governed by condominium rules, while life together is regulated by a written agreement between residents.
